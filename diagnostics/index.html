Our Services

Diagnostic Services We Offer

Accurate diagnostics form the foundation of effective medical care. Our diagnostic services support doctors in identifying and managing a wide range of health conditions.

Laboratory

Our laboratory provides diagnostic tests that help doctors assess and monitor health conditions. From routine blood work to specialised investigations, our laboratory services support timely and informed medical decisions.

X-Ray

X-Ray is an essential diagnostic imaging service used to examine bones, chest, and certain internal structures. Our X-Ray facility helps doctors quickly evaluate injuries, infections, and other conditions requiring imaging support.

Ultrasound

Ultrasound is a safe, non-invasive diagnostic imaging service that uses sound waves to create images of internal body structures. It is commonly used to examine abdominal organs, pregnancy, and other conditions requiring detailed imaging.
