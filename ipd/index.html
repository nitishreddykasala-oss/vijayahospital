Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about inpatient care at Vijaya Hospital

How many beds does Vijaya Hospital have?

Vijaya Hospital has 30 beds available in the inpatient department, along with rooms and wards to accommodate admitted patients.

Are rooms and wards available for admission?

Yes, Vijaya Hospital has rooms and wards available for patients who require admission. For availability and details, please call us at 9919913369.

How can I get admitted to the inpatient department?

For inpatient admission, you can visit Vijaya Hospital directly or call us at 9919913369. Our medical team will assess your condition and guide you through the admission process.

Do I need an appointment before admission?

For planned admissions, it is recommended to contact us in advance. For emergency situations, you can walk in directly. You can also book an appointment through our website for a consultation.
