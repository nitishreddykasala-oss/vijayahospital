About OPD

OPD Services at Vijaya Hospital, Shankerpally

The Outpatient Department (OPD) at Vijaya Hospital provides consultations for patients who do not require overnight hospitalisation. Patients can visit the OPD to consult with doctors, undergo preliminary examinations, receive prescriptions, and get referrals for further investigation or treatment if needed.

Vijaya Hospital is open 24 hours to support patients with their healthcare needs. The hospital is located at 4-23/A/1, Fathepur(V), Beside HP Petrol Pump, Chevella Road, Shankerpally – 501203.

OPD Consultations

What to Expect During Your OPD Visit

The OPD at Vijaya Hospital is designed to provide patients with convenient access to medical consultations.

Walk-in Consultations

Patients can visit the OPD during hospital hours for walk-in consultations with available doctors.

Appointment Booking

Schedule your OPD visit in advance by calling us or reaching out via WhatsApp.

Prescription & Referrals

After consultation, patients receive prescriptions and referrals for any further diagnostic tests or treatment as needed.

24-Hour Availability

Vijaya Hospital is open 24 hours. Patients can seek medical support at any time of the day or night.

OPD Process

Steps for Your OPD Visit

Visiting the OPD at Vijaya Hospital is straightforward. Here is a general overview of the process:

Step 1: Arrive at the hospital or call to schedule an appointment
Step 2: Register at the reception with your details
Step 3: Consult with the doctor regarding your health concerns
Step 4: Receive prescriptions, referrals, or further guidance from the doctor
